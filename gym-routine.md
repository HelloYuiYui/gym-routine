Monday: Upper Structure & Speed
Goal: Bone density, Upper Body Mass, Grip, and High-Heart Rate Finish. Duration: 2 Hours
1. The Warm-Up & Mobility (10–15 mins)
Dynamic: Arm circles, torso twists.
Shoulder Dislocates (Band Pass-Throughs): 2 sets × 10 reps (Mobility Insert).
Wall Angels: 2 sets × 10 reps (Mobility Insert).
Goblet Squat: 1 set × 8 reps.
KB Swing: 1 set × 15 reps.
Overhead Squat: 2 sets × 8 reps (Empty bar/PVC pipe).
2. The Strength Work (approx. 75–90 mins)
Bench Press: 5 sets × 5 reps
Focus: Pinch shoulder blades back. Feet driven into the floor.
Overhead Press (OHP): 3 sets × 6–8 reps
Focus: Squeeze glutes and abs. Lock out fully.
Pendlay Row (or DB Row): 4 sets × 8–12 reps
Focus: Explosive pull, controlled lower.
Tip: Use Lifting Straps if your forearms are tired from the curls.
Inclined Curls: 3 sets × 10–12 reps
Focus: Elbow health and grip strength.
Note: Do not fail on grip here; save some for the rows.
Assisted Pull-Up: 3 sets × 8–10 reps
Focus: Full stretch at the bottom.
Dead Hangs: 2 sets × Max Time (30s+)
Focus: Decompress spine.
3. Cool Down Mobility (5 mins)
Sleeper Stretch: 2 sets × 30s per side (Do NOT do this before lifting).
4. The Run (10 mins)
Position: LAST
Protocol: "Fastest Mile" Attempt. (alternating weeks)
Goal: Max effort (HR 180+). Empty the tank.


Wednesday: Lower Power & Volume
Goal: Raw Strength, Mass, and Hip Health. Duration: 2 Hours
1. The Warm-Up & Mobility (15 mins)
90/90 Hip Switch: 2 mins continuous flow (Mobility Insert).
Hip CARs (Standing): 3 slow circles per leg (Mobility Insert).
Bodyweight Cossack Squat: 2 sets × 6 per side (Mobility Insert).
Goblet Squat: 2 sets × 8 reps (Sit deep).
KB Swing: 2 sets × 15 reps (Wake up in hamstrings).
2. The Strength Work (approx. 80–90 mins)
Sumo Deadlift: 5 sets × 3 reps (Max) [Week A] 5 x 5 [Week B]
Rule: RESET every rep. Stand up, breathe, re-brace.
Speed squat 6×3 @ ~55–65% (fast, perfect reps)
Alternate Weeks
Week A: Front Squat – 3 sets × 6 reps
Week B: Zercher Squat – 3 sets × 6 reps
Close-Grip Bench Press (Pause mixed): 3 sets × 10 reps (5 reps normal 5 reps 1–2 sec pause)
Focus: Hands shoulder-width. Elbows tucked.
Leg Extension 3 sets × 10–12 reps
Leg Curl: 3 sets × 10–12 reps
3. The Run (10 mins)
Position: LAST
Protocol: HIIT Walk/Jog Intervals.
Goal: HR ~150. Hard walk (incline) mixed with jog. Do not max sprint.

Friday: Isometric Tendon & Capacity
Goal: Tendon Healing, Core Stability, Loaded Mobility. Duration: 2 Hours
1. The Run (10 mins)
Position: FIRST
Protocol: Moderate Steady State.
Goal: Consistent pace (Between Mon and Wed intensity).
2. The Warm-Up (10 mins)
Goblet Squat: 1 set × 8 reps.
KB Swing: 1 set × 15 reps.
Mobility Flow: Quick 90/90s + Shoulder Dislocates combined.
3. The Isometric Work (approx. 60–75 mins) Progression: Start 30s. Work to 90s. Then add load/intensity.
Back Squat: 5 sets × 8 reps [Week A] 5 x 5 (Max) [Week B]
Isometrics
Block A (Ankle): 3 sets × 30–45s
Standing Calf Raise Iso: 
Bent-Knee Calf Raise Iso
Block B (Adductor / Groin Tendon): Copenhagen Plank: 4 sets × 20–40s per side  
Intensity: RPE 7–9 
Progression: short-lever Copenhagen → long-lever Copenhagen → weighted Copenhagen 
Block C (Hinge / Proximal Hamstring Tendon + CNS Pull ): Mid-Thigh Pull Iso against immovable pins/strap : 5 sets × 3–6s overcoming iso    
Intensity: RPE 9–10
Rest 90–120s 
Setup: bar or strap at mid-thigh height, brace like a deadlift, pull violently but without jerking your spine. 
Block D (Upper Tendon / Pressing Lockout ): Close-Grip Bench Pin Press Iso or Push-Up Iso Hold: 4 sets × 3–6s overcoming iso    
Intensity: RPE 9
Block E (Knee): Spanish Squat Hold (or Leg Ext Iso): 5 sets × 30–45s
Intensity: RPE 9.
Block F (Core): Pallof Press Hold: 4 sets × 20–45s
4. The Finisher (15–20 mins)
Block G: Farmer’s Carry Intervals
Protocol: 8–12 Rounds. Carry 30s / Rest 60s. Go Heavy.
Use “double progression”
Pick a rep range (e.g., 5×5, or 3×6–8)


When you hit all reps with clean form and consistent bar speed: add weight next week.


If you miss: keep weight, get the reps next week (or drop 2.5–5% and rebuild).
